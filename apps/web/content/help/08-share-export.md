When a piece is ready for other eyes, Litma can give it a reading link, a PDF, a Word file and more. Versions keep every earlier state of your work safe.

## Share a reading link {#share-link}

Send someone a link so they can read your work in their browser, without an account.

1. In the editor choose **Share** at the top.
2. Tick **Anyone with the link can read**. The link appears below it.
3. Choose **Copy** and send the link.

The reader sees your work on one clean continuous sheet, with an outline beside it on wide screens and a bar that shows how far they have read. They can't change anything. Untick it any time and the link stops working.

> **Phone:** Open a work, tap **More → Share & export** and switch on **Anyone with the link can read**. The share button next to the link sends it straight to an app on your phone.

## Export a PDF, Word file or text {#export}

![The Export menu](shots/export.webp)

Choose **Export** at the top of the editor:

- **PDF, print-ready pages:** opens the print view with your work laid out on pages. Choose **Save as PDF**, then in the print window pick **Save as PDF**, margins **None**, and turn on **Background graphics**.
- **Quick print** (<kbd>Ctrl</kbd> + <kbd>P</kbd>): prints the pages straight away.
- **Word (.docx):** opens in Microsoft Word, Google Docs or LibreOffice with your styles, sizes, colours and footnotes.
- **Markdown (.md)**, **Plain text (.txt)** and **Web page (.html)** for other apps and websites.

> **Phone:** **More → Share & export** offers PDF, Word and Text under **Export**, and opens your phone's share sheet so you can save the file or send it.

## Version history {#versions}

Litma keeps earlier versions of your work, so you can always go back.

![Version history](shots/history.webp)

- Litma saves a version for you every 10 minutes while you write.
- Press <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> + <kbd>S</kbd> (or open **Version history** on the rail and choose **Save**) to keep a version now. Give it a name, like "Before the big edit", so it's easy to find.
- To go back, open **Version history**, find the version and choose **Restore this version** (the arrow), then confirm. Your current text is saved first as a version called "Before restore", so restoring never loses anything.

> **Good to know:** Working offline? When your text and the copy on the server both changed, Litma keeps the server's text as a version called "Before offline changes", so nothing is lost. See [Working offline](#offline).
