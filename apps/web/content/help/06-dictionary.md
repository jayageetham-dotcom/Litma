Litma has a Malayalam ↔ English dictionary and thesaurus built in, so you can check a word without leaving your page.

## Look up a word {#look-up}

On the website and desktop app, open **Dictionary** at the top of any page (the book icon), or inside a work choose **Dictionary & thesaurus** on the right-hand rail.

1. Type a word in Malayalam, in English, or in Manglish (`mazha` finds മഴ).
2. Words appear under the box as you type: English words that start with those letters, and for Manglish the Malayalam words it may stand for (`kadal` offers കടൽ, കടൽക്കര…). Pick one with a click, or with <kbd>↑</kbd> <kbd>↓</kbd> and <kbd>Enter</kbd>.
3. You see its meanings, the kind of word (noun, verb…) and synonyms (പര്യായങ്ങൾ), best first.

Inside the editor you can also select a word and press <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> + <kbd>Shift</kbd> + <kbd>L</kbd> to look it up. Click a synonym to put it in place of the selected word.

> **Phone:** Tap **Dictionary** in the bottom tab bar. Tap a meaning or synonym to look that word up; the **←** beside the word takes you back one word at a time.
>
> While writing, select a word and tap **Look up** in the bar that appears, or in the strip above the keyboard.

![The dictionary on the phone](shots/phone-dictionary.webp)

> **Good to know:** The dictionary works offline once you have used it, on the website and in the desktop app.

## Find rhymes (പ്രാസം) {#rhymes}

The rhyme finder suggests words from the dictionary that rhyme with a word or a line, with the laghu and guru of each word, so they fit the metre too.

![The rhyme finder beside a poem](shots/rhymes.webp "Rhymes for the line being written, with each word's laghu (∪) and guru (–).")

1. Inside a work, choose **Rhymes (പ്രാസം)** on the right-hand rail. Or open **Dictionary** and the **പ്രാസം** tab.
2. Pick the kind of rhyme:
   - **ദ്വിതീയാക്ഷരം**: the second akshara has the same consonant (കാലം, നീലം, ജാലം). Words whose first akshara has the same weight come first, as the classical rule asks.
   - **അന്ത്യപ്രാസം**: words that end in the same akshara (മഴയിൽ, പുഴയിൽ).
   - **ആദിപ്രാസം**: words that start with the same letter.
3. Click a word to put it in your text. In the Dictionary, click it to see its meaning.

In the editor the finder follows the line you are on. When that line is still empty it uses the line above, so the next line can rhyme with it. You can also type any word, in Malayalam or Manglish.

Tick **Same number of aksharas** or **Same laghu–guru pattern** to see only words that fit the same place in the line.

The finder shows 40 words at a time, best known first. Tap **Next set** under them for the next 40; after the last set it starts again from the first.

> **Phone:** In a work, tap the tools button and choose **Rhymes**. Tap a word to put it at the cursor. Or open **Dictionary** and the **പ്രാസം** tab; there a word opens its meaning.

> **Good to know:** The rhyme finder works offline once you have used it, like the dictionary.

## നാട്ടറിവ്: words of Kerala {#nattarivu}

നാട്ടറിവ് gathers everyday Kerala words into topics: family, food, festivals, arts, seasons and months, the house, nature and more. Each word has a plain English meaning, so it helps when you are looking for the right word or checking one you half remember.

![നാട്ടറിവ് topics](shots/nattarivu.webp)

1. Open the **Dictionary** and choose the **നാട്ടറിവ്** tab.
2. Pick a topic card.
3. To narrow a long topic, type in the filter box, in English, Malayalam or Manglish.
4. Click any word to open its full dictionary entry. A chip at the top takes you back to the topic.

![A നാട്ടറിവ് topic](shots/nattarivu-cat.webp)

## Saved words {#saved-words}

Keep the words you want to use later.

1. Look a word up.
2. Choose the bookmark button beside it.

Your saved words are listed under **Saved** in the Dictionary (beside the dictionary on wide screens). Inside a work, open **Saved words** on the rail and tap a word to put it in your text. Saved words follow your account to every device.
