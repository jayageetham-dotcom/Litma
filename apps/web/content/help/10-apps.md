Write on whichever device is near you. Your works are the same everywhere, and Litma keeps working when the internet doesn't.

## Install the desktop app {#desktop-app}

The desktop app is Litma in its own window, with its own icon in the Dock or taskbar. It looks and works like the website.

![The download page](shots/download.webp)

1. Go to **getlitma.in/download**. The page picks your computer's version for you.
2. Choose the download button.
3. Install it:
   - **Mac:** open the `.dmg` file and drag **Litma** into **Applications**. The first time, if your Mac asks, choose **Open**.
   - **Windows:** run the `.exe` installer. If Windows SmartScreen appears, choose **More info → Run anyway**.
   - **Linux:** make the `.AppImage` file executable (right-click → Properties → Allow executing) and open it.
4. Open Litma and sign in with the same account.

> **Desktop:** Help is always one click away: choose **Help → Litma Help** in the menu bar (or press <kbd>F1</kbd> on Windows and Linux), or **Settings → Help and guide**. The guides open in your web browser.

## Desktop app updates {#updates}

The desktop app checks for a new version every few hours. When one is ready, a small card says **Litma (version) is available**. Choose **Update**: it downloads in the background, then choose **Restart and update**. Your works are not touched. **Later** hides the card for now.

The website updates by itself. If a new version arrives while you are writing, a card offers **Reload now**; your writing is safe on the device.

The version you have is under **About** at the bottom of **Settings**.

## Litma on your phone {#phone-app}

The phone app is made for small screens, for iPhone and Android. It has the desk, every writing type, Manglish suggestions above the keyboard, Story World, the dictionary with നാട്ടറിവ്, books and covers, sharing and export, the AI companion, version history, and it works offline.

![The phone app's desk](shots/phone-desk.webp)
![Writing on the phone](shots/phone-editor.webp)
![The writing types on the phone](shots/phone-types.webp)

- The bottom tab bar has **Desk**, **Dictionary** and **Me**.
- In a work, the strip above the keyboard holds formatting, **Look up** and **Ask AI**. Tap **More** for Story World, version history, share and export, and page setup.
- Select text to get a small bar with Look up, Ask AI, bold, italic and Copy.
- **Settings → Help and guide** opens these guides in your phone's browser.

> **Note:** The phone app is on its way to the App Store and Google Play. Until then, the website works well in your phone's browser too.

## Working offline {#offline}

Litma keeps a copy of your works on each device, so you can write with no internet at all: on a train, on a flight, or when the wifi drops.

- Open and edit any work you have opened on this device before. Recent works are kept ready for you.
- Changes are saved on the device right away. A small pill says **Offline. Your writing is saved on this device.**
- When the connection comes back, Litma sends your changes in the order you made them. The pill says **Back online. Sending your changes…** and then **All changes synced**.
- The dictionary keeps the words you have looked up.

> **Good to know:** If the same work was changed on another device while you were offline, your offline text wins and the other text is kept as a version called "Before offline changes". Open [Version history](#versions) to compare or bring it back.

> **Note:** A work you have never opened on this device shows **Not on this device yet** while offline. Open it once while online; after that it opens offline too.
