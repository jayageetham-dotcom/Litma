DESCRIPTION
Litma is a writing desk for Malayalam literature. Write novels, short stories, poems, songs, screenplays, plays, essays, features and diaries, each with its own layout, on pages that look like the printed book.

TYPE THE WAY YOU THINK
• Type in Manglish and watch it turn into Malayalam script, with a row of spellings to pick from.
• Or type straight in Malayalam with the Mac's Malayalam keyboard or Litma's built-in InScript keyboard, or in English.
• Works without a connection. Your writing is kept on your Mac and syncs when you are back online.

A HOME FOR EVERY WRITING TYPE
• Novels and stories with chapters, drop caps and book-style paragraphs.
• Poems and songs with stanzas, refrain and metre marks (വൃത്തം).
• Screenplays and plays with scenes, acts, dialogue and a cast list.
• Essays and features with footnotes and references.
• A diary that adds today's date for each new entry.

WORDS AT YOUR FINGERTIPS
• A built-in Malayalam dictionary and thesaurus, with English meanings.
• നാട്ടറിവ്: Malayalam words from Kerala's life, food, festivals, arts and seasons, gathered by theme.
• Rhymes (പ്രാസം) for poets and lyricists.
• A spelling check that knows how Malayalam words join.
• Save the words you love and use them later.

PLAN YOUR STORY
• Story World (കഥാലോകം): characters, places, objects, a plot board and a timeline beside your text.
• Version history, so you can go back to an earlier draft.
• Writing goals, a daily reminder, a writing timer and your streak.

FROM DRAFT TO BOOK
• Gather works into a book with contents, details and a cover designed in the app.
• Export to PDF or Word, or share a link so others can read your work.

AN AI COMPANION, ON YOUR TERMS
Ask for feedback, a grammar check or ideas using your own Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini key. Your writing goes to the AI only when you ask.

PRIVATE BY DEFAULT
Your writing stays private unless you share a link. No ads. You can switch off usage statistics, back up your works, or delete your account at any time from Settings.

One account works on your Mac, on iPhone and iPad, at getlitma.in and in the desktop apps for Windows and Linux.
