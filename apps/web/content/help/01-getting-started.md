Litma is a writing desk made for Malayalam. These guides take you from a new account to your first saved page.

## What Litma is {#what-is-litma}

Litma is a place to write Malayalam novels, stories, poems, screenplays, plays and more. You can type in Manglish (English letters that turn into Malayalam), with a Malayalam keyboard, or in English, and your work is laid out on real pages as you write.

![The Litma desk with a few works](shots/desk.webp "Your desk (എഴുത്തുമേശ) keeps every work in one place.")

### One account, every device

Litma works in three places, and they all share the same account and the same works:

- **The website** at getlitma.in, in any modern browser.
- **The desktop app** for Mac, Windows and Linux. See [Install the desktop app](#desktop-app).
- **The phone app** for iPhone and Android. See [Litma on your phone](#phone-app).

A change you make on one device shows up on the others. If you lose your connection, Litma keeps writing on the device and sends your changes later. See [Working offline](#offline).

### What you can do

- Write in ten writing types (സാഹിത്യരൂപങ്ങൾ), each with its own page layout. See [Writing types](/help/?c=types).
- Keep notes on characters, places and plot in [Story World](#story-world).
- Look words up in the [dictionary](#look-up) and browse [നാട്ടറിവ്](#nattarivu).
- Collect works into a [book with a cover](#make-a-book) and export a print-ready PDF.
- Ask an [AI companion](#ai-companion) for help, using your own AI account.

## Create your account and sign in {#sign-in}

You need an account so your works are saved safely and sync between your devices. It is free.

![The Create account page](shots/signup.webp)

### Create an account

1. Go to **getlitma.in** and choose **Create account**.
2. Type your name or pen name, your email and a password (type it twice).
3. Choose **Create account**. Litma sends you an email.
4. Open the email and choose the link in it to confirm your address. Then sign in.

Or choose **Continue with Google** to use your Google account; there is no email to confirm.

> **Phone:** The phone app also offers **Continue with Apple** on iPhone.

### Sign in

Choose **Sign in** at the top of the home page, type your email and password, and you land on your desk. Use the eye button in the password field to see what you typed.

### Forgot your password?

1. On the Sign in page choose **Forgot password?**
2. Type your email and choose **Send the link**.
3. Open the email from Litma (from no-reply@getlitma.in) and choose the link.
4. Choose a new password. The link works in any browser, even a different one from where you asked for it.

> **Note:** No email? Look in your spam or promotions folder, and check that you typed the address you signed up with. Still nothing after a few minutes? Write to support@getlitma.in.

## Your desk {#desk}

The desk (എഴുത്തുമേശ) is the first page you see after signing in. It shows your works, your books and the trash.

![The desk](shots/desk.webp)

### What's on the desk

- **Works, Books, Trash:** the three tabs. Works are your writings; Books collect works into a printable book; Trash holds deleted works.
- **Word counts:** how many works and words you have, words written today, and your writing streak (days in a row you have written).
- **Search titles and text:** finds a work by its title or by any words inside it.
- **Sort:** Last edited, Title, or Longest.
- **Writing type chips:** show only novels, only poems and so on.
- **New work** and **Import:** start writing, or bring in a text file.

Each card shows the writing type with its coloured dot, the title, the first lines and the word count. Click a card to open the work. Point at a card to see more actions, such as **Duplicate** and **Move to trash**.

> **Phone:** On the phone the desk has a round **+** button at the bottom for a new work, and a tab bar with Desk, Dictionary and Me.

![The desk on the phone app](shots/phone-desk.webp)

## Start your first work {#first-work}

Every work starts with a writing type (സാഹിത്യരൂപം). It sets the page, the fonts and the blocks you can use, like chapters for a novel or scenes for a screenplay.

1. On the desk choose **New work**.
2. Pick a writing type. Each one shows a real first page so you can see what you get.
   ![Choosing a writing type](shots/chooser.webp "Ten writing types, each with a preview of its first page.")
3. The editor opens with a template: grey prompts such as ശീർഷകം (title) and എഴുതുന്ന ആളുടെ പേര് (author) show where things go. Click a prompt and type over it.
4. Press <kbd>Enter</kbd> at the end of a template line to jump to the next empty one.

Litma saves as you type. There is no Save button; the line under the title says **Saved** (or **Saved in this browser** when you use Litma without an account).

> **Tip:** Your pen name or profile name fills the author line of every new work, so you don't type it each time. Change it in [your profile](#profile).

> **Good to know:** A new work that you leave without typing anything is thrown away, so the desk doesn't fill up with empty pages.

### Rename a work

Click the work's title at the top left of the editor and type a new name. The title on the page (the ശീർഷകം line) is part of your text and can be different.

## Import a text or Markdown file {#import}

Bring in writing you started elsewhere, from a `.txt` or `.md` file.

1. On the desk choose **Import**.
2. Pick the file.
3. Choose the writing type it should become.

Litma turns the file into a work:

- Lines starting with `#` become titles, and lines starting with `##` become chapters.
- In a screenplay, lines like `Name: what they say` become dialogue.
- Everything else becomes ordinary paragraphs.

> **Tip:** For a Word file, save it as plain text (.txt) first, or copy the text and paste it into a new work. Pasted text keeps its paragraphs.

## Move a work to the trash and back {#trash}

Deleting a work moves it to the trash first, so a slip of the hand never loses anything.

![The Trash tab](shots/desk-trash.webp)

1. On the desk point at a work, choose **Move to trash** and confirm. (On the phone, swipe the work or use its menu; a note with **Undo** appears for a moment.)
2. To get it back, open the **Trash** tab and choose **Restore**.
3. To delete one work for good, choose **Delete forever** on it. **Empty trash** deletes everything in the trash.

> **Note:** Delete forever also removes the work's versions and Story World. It can't be undone.
