Every work in Litma has a writing type (സാഹിത്യരൂപം). It decides the page layout, the fonts and the styles on the toolbar, so a screenplay looks like a screenplay and a poem like a poem. These guides show what each one gives you.

## Choosing a writing type {#choose-type}

When you choose **New work**, Litma shows ten writing types with a preview of their first page:

| Writing type | Good for |
| --- | --- |
| നോവൽ · Novel | Long fiction in chapters |
| ചെറുകഥ · Short story | A story in one piece |
| കവിത · Poem | Verse, with metre marks when you want them |
| തിരക്കഥ · Screenplay | Film scripts: scenes, action and dialogue |
| ഫീച്ചർ · Feature writing | Magazine and newspaper features |
| ലേഖനം · Essay / article | Essays and articles with footnotes |
| നാടകം · Drama / play | Plays: cast list, acts, scenes and stage directions |
| ഗാനം · Song lyrics | Songs with പല്ലവി and അനുപല്ലവി |
| ഡയറി · Diary / memoir | Dated entries |
| ശൂന്യം · Blank page | Anything else, with no template |

> **Good to know:** The writing type can't be changed after you start. If you picked the wrong one, start a new work of the right type and paste your text in.

## Novels and short stories {#novel}

![A novel in the editor](shots/editor.webp)

- The template gives a title, the author line (രചന), a മേൽക്കുറിപ്പ് (epigraph) and the first chapter.
- Use **Chapter** from the style menu for each new chapter. Chapters appear in the outline, and you can have them numbered (**Page & type → Number the chapters**).
- The first letter after a chapter is drawn larger, like in printed books.
- **Scene break** (⁂) separates scenes inside a chapter.
- Paragraphs are indented like a printed book, except the first one after a chapter or a break. An empty line works as a quiet section break.

## Poems and song lyrics {#poem}

![A poem with metre marks](shots/poem-metre.webp "Metre marks show the akshara count and the laghu and guru of each line.")

- Each line is its own line; press <kbd>Enter</kbd> for the next. An empty line starts a new stanza.
- Poem lines can be centred: **Page & type → Lines → Centred**.
- Song lyrics have പല്ലവി, അനുപല്ലവി and ചരണം labels. Press <kbd>Enter</kbd> on a label to move to its first line.

### Metre marks (വൃത്തം)

Choose **വൃത്തം** on the toolbar, or press <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> + <kbd>Alt</kbd> + <kbd>V</kbd>. Litma shows how many aksharas each line has and marks every syllable as laghu (short) or guru (long), so you can check the metre as you write. The marks never print. Choose it again to hide them.

## Screenplays {#screenplay}

![A screenplay](shots/screenplay.webp)

A screenplay is written in blocks. The toolbar shows them as buttons: **Scene**, **Time**, **Cast**, **Action**, **Dialogue** and **Transition**.

1. Start a scene with **Scene**. Open **Scene…** on the toolbar to pick **Int.** or **Ext.** and type the place.
2. Add the **Time of day** (പകൽ, രാത്രി…) and the **Cast** in the scene. **Fill the cast list** in **Scene…** adds everyone who speaks in that scene.
3. Write what we see as **Action**.
4. For a line of dialogue, choose **Dialogue**, type the character's name, press <kbd>Enter</kbd> and type what they say.

Scenes are numbered for you (രംഗം 1 or Scene 1, chosen in **Page & type**) and listed in the outline.

## Plays {#drama}

![A play with a cast list](shots/drama.webp)

- **Cast list:** each entry has a name and a short description. The **Cast list** button adds your Story World characters (add them first in Story World → Characters).
- **Act** and **Scene** blocks are numbered for you and shown in both languages in the menus (അങ്കം · Act). Scene numbers start again in each act.
- **Stage direction** is set in italics between brackets.
- Dialogue works like in a screenplay: the name, then <kbd>Enter</kbd>, then the speech.

## Features and essays {#feature}

![A feature with a footnote](shots/feature.webp)

- Features have a kicker (the small line above the title), a standfirst (the short lead under the title), a byline and pull quotes.
- **Footnotes:** put the cursor where the note belongs, choose **Footnote** on the toolbar, type the note and choose **Add footnote**. A small number appears in the text and the notes are listed at the end of the work, in the PDF and in the Word file. Click a number to edit or remove its note.

## Diary and memoir {#diary}

![A diary with dated entries](shots/diary.webp)

Choose **New entry** on the toolbar to add today's date at the end and start writing under it. The outline lists every date, so you can jump back to any day.
