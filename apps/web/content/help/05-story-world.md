Story World (കഥാലോകം) is a notebook that belongs to one work. It keeps everything you need to remember about the people and places in it, right next to the page.

## Open Story World {#story-world}

There are two ways in:

- **The side panel:** choose **Story World** on the editor's right-hand rail. It lists your characters, places and objects, with a box for quick notes, while you keep writing.
- **The full view:** choose **Open Story World** at the top of that panel for cards, the plot board, the timeline and reports.

![Story World beside the page](shots/story-panel.webp)

![The full Story World view](shots/story-world.webp "Characters, places, objects, plot board, timeline, research and reports, each in its own tab.")

The tabs depend on the writing type:

| Writing type | Tabs |
| --- | --- |
| Novel, short story | Characters, Places, Objects, Plot board, Timeline, Research |
| Screenplay, play | Characters, Locations (sets), Props, Beat board, Timeline, Notes |
| Poem, song lyrics | Images & ideas, Structure, Voices |
| Feature, essay | People, Places, Sources, Outline |
| Diary | People, Places, Timeline, Notes |

Every writing type also has **Reports**.

> **Phone:** Open a work, tap **Story** in the tab bar. The same tabs are there, one under the other.

![Story World on the phone](shots/phone-story.webp)

## Characters, places and objects {#story-cards}

1. In Story World choose the tab, for example **Characters**.
2. Choose **Add character** (or Add place, Add object).
3. Fill in what you know: name, a one-line summary, role, age, occupation, what they want, and any notes. Leave the rest empty.
4. Close the card. It is saved as you type.

A card you close without writing anything in it is removed, so empty cards don't pile up.

> **Tip:** Characters you add here can fill a play's cast list or a screenplay's scenes with one click. See [Plays](#drama).

## Plot board and timeline {#plot-board}

![The plot board](shots/story-beats.webp "Plan the story in columns and move cards as it changes.")

The **plot board** (Beat board in a screenplay) holds the story's moments as cards in columns, such as beginning, middle and end.

- **Add card** at the bottom of a column. Give it a title, a line about what happens, a status (Idea, Drafting, Written) and a colour.
- Drag cards between columns, or use the arrow buttons on the card (they are always shown on touch screens).
- Rename a column by clicking its name. Add columns, drag them into order, and remove a column once it is empty.

The **timeline** lists events in the order you add them: when it happens, who is there and where.

![The timeline](shots/story-timeline.webp)

## Research and reports {#story-reports}

- **Research** keeps notes, sources and anything you looked up.
- **Reports** reads your work and shows how many words each chapter or section has and how often each character and place is mentioned in the text. Use it to find a character who disappears for half the book.
