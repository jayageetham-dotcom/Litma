Litma can work with AI, but only when you ask, and only with your own AI account. Nothing is sent to an AI unless you choose a task.

## The AI companion {#ai-companion}

Open **AI companion** on the editor's right-hand rail.

![The AI companion](shots/ai.webp)

- **For the whole work:** Continue writing, Dialogue ideas, Describe the scene, Title ideas, Summarise and Editor's notes. They use the text around your cursor.
- **For selected text:** select a passage first, then Rewrite, Polish language, Proofread, Synonyms, Rhymes (പ്രാസം), or translate it to Malayalam or to English.
- **Ask in your own words:** type any question about your work.

The answer appears in the panel. Choose **Insert** to put it in your text, or copy just the part you want.

**Answer with** lets you pick Claude, GPT or Gemini, whichever you have a key for.

## Add your own AI key {#ai-keys}

The AI companion runs on your own AI account, so you pay your AI provider directly and there are no shared limits.

1. Get an API key from Anthropic (Claude), OpenAI (GPT) or Google AI Studio (Gemini). Settings has a link to each one.
2. In Litma open **Settings → Your AI keys**.
3. Paste the key in the box for that provider and choose **Save key**. You can also set the model and mark one provider as **Default**.

Keys are stored encrypted, only for your account, and are only used when you ask the companion something.

> **Note:** If the provider says the key has no credit or isn't valid, the companion tells you in plain words and offers the next way below.

## Use your Claude plan instead of a key {#open-claude}

A Claude Pro or Max plan doesn't include API credit, but you can still use it with Litma:

1. Connect Litma to Claude once (see [Connect an AI assistant](#mcp)).
2. In the AI companion, pick a task. Without a key, Litma offers **Open Claude**.
3. Claude opens with a ready request: your work's title, the task and the selected passage.
4. Claude reads your work through the Litma connector and sends its suggestions back to Litma.
5. Back in Litma, open the **Assistant inbox** on the rail to read them.

## Connect an AI assistant (MCP) {#mcp}

Litma can be connected to any AI assistant that supports MCP (the Model Context Protocol), like Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor or VS Code. Once connected, the assistant can read and write your works, keep Story World up to date, look words up in the dictionary and prepare PDFs, but only for your account.

![Connecting an AI assistant in Settings](shots/settings-mcp.webp)

1. Open **Settings → Connect AI assistants (MCP)**.
2. Under **Access tokens**, type a name (for example "Claude") and choose **Create token**. Copy the address shown. It includes your token, so keep it private like a password.
3. In your assistant, add a custom connector with that address. For Claude: **Settings → Connectors → Add custom connector**, name it "Litma", paste the address and choose **Add**. If Claude says it couldn't verify the server, choose **Continue anyway**.

Settings shows the exact steps for Claude, ChatGPT, Claude Code, Claude Desktop, Cursor and VS Code.

To disconnect an assistant, delete its token in Settings. It stops working at once.

> **Tip:** Assistants are told to suggest rather than change. Their suggestions land in your Assistant inbox, where you accept or ignore each one.

## The Assistant inbox {#inbox}

Open **Assistant inbox** on the editor's rail to see suggestions an assistant sent for this work. Each one shows the suggested text and a note on why. **Replace** or **Insert** puts it in your text, **Copy** copies it, and **Dismiss** removes it from the inbox.
