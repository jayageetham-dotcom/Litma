The editor is where you write. It looks like a sheet of paper and grows page by page as you type.

## A tour of the editor {#editor-tour}

Open any work from the desk and you are in the editor.

![The editor with a novel open](shots/editor.webp "The editor: toolbar on top, the outline on the left, tools on the right.")

- **Top bar:** back to the desk, the work's title (click to rename), **Export**, **Share**, dark mode, keyboard shortcuts (**?**) and focus mode.
- **Toolbar:** typing mode, paragraph style, font, size, bold and the other text tools.
- **Outline (left):** every title, chapter, scene and subhead. Click one to jump to it.
- **The page:** your writing on real A4 sheets (or the paper size you choose).
- **Rail (right):** AI companion, Dictionary & thesaurus, Saved words, Assistant inbox, Story World, Version history, Malayalam keyboard, Page & type and Typewriter scrolling. Point at the rail to see the names.
- **Status bar (bottom):** words, characters, pages, reading time, typing mode and zoom.

> **Phone:** On the phone the writing fills the screen as one long sheet. While you type, a strip above the keyboard holds the formatting buttons; when the keyboard is closed, a tab bar gives you Format, Contents, Story, Dictionary and More.

![The editor's tools on the phone](shots/phone-tools.webp)

## Paragraph styles {#styles}

Each writing type comes with its own styles: a novel has Title, Author, മേൽക്കുറിപ്പ് (epigraph), Chapter and Scene break; a screenplay has scene headings and dialogue. Styles keep the look consistent and fill the outline.

![The paragraph style menu](shots/styles.webp "The style menu lists the styles of this writing type.")

1. Click in the line you want to change.
2. Open the style menu on the toolbar (it shows the current style, like **Body text**).
3. Pick a style. Choose **Body text** to go back to a plain paragraph.

> **Tip:** Chapters can be numbered for you (അദ്ധ്യായം 1, 2…). Turn it on in **Page & type → Number the chapters**.

## Bold, italic, size and fonts {#text-format}

Select some text, then use the toolbar:

- **B**, *I*, U, strikethrough, superscript and subscript.
- **Font for selected text** changes the font of just the selection. The document's main font is in [Page & type](#page-setup).
- **− 12 +** makes the selected text smaller or bigger. **Automatic** goes back to the size the style gives.
- **Clear formatting** removes all of the above.
- **Link** turns the selection into a web link.

Bold also works on lines that are bold by style, like titles: press <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> + <kbd>B</kbd> to make a title regular weight.

## Text colour and highlight {#colours}

![The text colour picker](shots/colour.webp)

1. Select the text.
2. Choose **Text colour** (the A with a line) or **Highlight** (the marker).
3. Pick a swatch, or type your own colour code and choose **Apply**. Colours you used lately are kept below.

**Automatic** removes the colour; **None** removes a highlight. The swatches are chosen to stay readable on white paper. In dark mode Litma shows coloured text a little lighter so it stays readable, without changing the colour that prints.

## Alignment, lists and quotations {#alignment}

The toolbar also has left, centre, right and justify, bulleted and numbered lists, a quotation block and a scene break (⁂).

> **Good to know:** Body text is ragged-right (lines end where they end), like a printed literary book, so short Malayalam lines never get wide gaps between words. Choose **Justify** only if you want even edges.

In novels and stories, paragraphs start with a small indent like a printed book. The first paragraph of a chapter, the first after a scene break, and the paragraph after an empty line sit at the margin. An empty line works as a quiet section break.

## Pages and Insert page {#pages}

Your text flows from page to page by itself, like in Google Docs. A page exists only while there is text on it, so when you delete the last words of a page, the page goes away.

![Insert page between two pages](shots/insert-page.webp "Point at the gap between two pages to see Insert page.")

To start a fresh page (for example before a new part):

1. Point at the gap between two pages. An **Insert page** button appears (on touch screens it is always there).
2. Click it. A blank page is added at that point.

To remove an inserted page, put the cursor at the start of the page after it and press <kbd>Backspace</kbd>. On an empty inserted page, <kbd>Backspace</kbd> removes the whole page.

## Page & type: paper, margins and fonts {#page-setup}

Open **Page & type** on the right-hand rail to set up the whole work.

![The Page & type panel](shots/page-type.webp)

- **Paper size:** A4 (the default), A5, US Letter or Legal.
- **Margins** in millimetres.
- **Document font**, **Size** and **Line spacing** for the body text.
- **Running header:** a line printed at the top of every page.
- **Word goal for this work:** a target you can see in the status bar.
- Choices for the writing type, like **Number the chapters**, numbered scene labels in Malayalam or English (രംഗം 1 or Scene 1), or centred poem lines.

## Find and replace {#find}

1. Press <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> + <kbd>F</kbd> (<kbd>⌘</kbd> + <kbd>F</kbd> on a Mac).
2. Type what to look for. Every match is highlighted and the count shows (1/3).
3. Use the arrows (or <kbd>Enter</kbd>) to go from one to the next. Tick **Aa** to match capital letters exactly.
4. To replace, type the new text in **Replace with** and choose **Replace** (this one) or **All**.

![Find and replace](shots/find.webp)

## Focus mode, typewriter scrolling and zoom {#focus}

- **Focus mode** (the arrows at the top right, or <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> + <kbd>Shift</kbd> + <kbd>F</kbd>) hides everything but the page. Press <kbd>Esc</kbd> to come back.
- **Typewriter scrolling** (on the rail) keeps the line you are typing in the middle of the screen.
- **Zoom** sits at the bottom right: **−**, the percentage (click to reset) and **+**.
- **Dark mode** (the moon at the top) is easier on the eyes at night. Your pages still print black on white.

![Focus mode](shots/focus.webp "Focus mode: just you and the page.")

## Keyboard shortcuts {#shortcuts}

Choose **?** at the top of the editor, or press <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> + <kbd>/</kbd>, to see this list in the app. On a Mac use <kbd>⌘</kbd> for <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>.

| Keys | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> + <kbd>M</kbd> | Switch Manglish → മലയാളം → English |
| <kbd>Space</kbd>, <kbd>Enter</kbd> or punctuation | Take the highlighted Malayalam word |
| <kbd>1</kbd> to <kbd>6</kbd>, <kbd>↑</kbd> <kbd>↓</kbd> | Pick another suggestion |
| <kbd>Esc</kbd> | Keep the English word |
| <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> + <kbd>Shift</kbd> + <kbd>M</kbd> | Turn selected Manglish into Malayalam |
| <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> + <kbd>S</kbd> | Save a named version now |
| <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> + <kbd>F</kbd> | Find and replace |
| <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> + <kbd>P</kbd> | Print or save as PDF |
| <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> + <kbd>Shift</kbd> + <kbd>F</kbd> | Focus mode |
| <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> + <kbd>Shift</kbd> + <kbd>L</kbd> | Dictionary and thesaurus for the selected word |
| <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> + <kbd>Alt</kbd> + <kbd>V</kbd> | Show or hide metre marks (poems, lyrics) |
| <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> + <kbd>B</kbd> / <kbd>I</kbd> / <kbd>U</kbd> | Bold, italic, underline |
| <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> + <kbd>Z</kbd>, <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> + <kbd>Shift</kbd> + <kbd>Z</kbd> | Undo, redo |
| <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> + <kbd>/</kbd> | Show the shortcuts |
