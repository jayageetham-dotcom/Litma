Litma has a Malayalam ↔ English dictionary and thesaurus built in, from the Olam open dictionary, so you can check a word without leaving your page.

## Look up a word {#look-up}

On the website and desktop app, open **Dictionary** at the top of any page (the book icon), or inside a work choose **Dictionary & thesaurus** on the right-hand rail.

1. Type a word in Malayalam, in English, or in Manglish (`mazha` finds മഴ).
2. Pick it from the list.
3. You see its meanings, the kind of word (noun, verb…) and synonyms (പര്യായങ്ങൾ), best first.

Inside the editor you can also select a word and press <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> + <kbd>Shift</kbd> + <kbd>L</kbd> to look it up. Click a synonym to put it in place of the selected word.

> **Phone:** Tap **Dictionary** in the bottom tab bar. While writing, select a word and tap **Look up** in the bar that appears, or in the strip above the keyboard.

![The dictionary on the phone](shots/phone-dictionary.webp)

> **Good to know:** The dictionary works offline once you have used it, on the website and in the desktop app.

## നാട്ടറിവ്: words of Kerala {#nattarivu}

നാട്ടറിവ് gathers everyday Kerala words into topics: family, food, festivals, arts, seasons and months, the house, nature and more. Each word has a plain English meaning, so it helps when you are looking for the right word or checking one you half remember.

![നാട്ടറിവ് topics](shots/nattarivu.webp)

1. Open the **Dictionary** and choose the **നാട്ടറിവ്** tab.
2. Pick a topic card.
3. Click any word to open its full dictionary entry. A chip at the top takes you back to the topic.

![A നാട്ടറിവ് topic](shots/nattarivu-cat.webp)

## Saved words {#saved-words}

Keep the words you want to use later.

1. Look a word up.
2. Choose the bookmark button beside it.

Your saved words are listed under **Saved** in the Dictionary (beside the dictionary on wide screens). Inside a work, open **Saved words** on the rail and tap a word to put it in your text. Saved words follow your account to every device.
