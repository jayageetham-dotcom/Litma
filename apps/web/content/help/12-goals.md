Write a little every day: set a goal, keep a streak going, write in timed rounds, get a gentle reminder and see how your writing goes. Everything here is free and works on the website, the desktop app and the phone.

## Goals and streaks {#goals}

Open **Goals and statistics** from your picture menu (top right), from **Settings**, or from your profile.

![Goals and statistics](shots/goals.webp "Today's words and streak, your goals, the daily reminder and your statistics.")

- **Words a day** and **Words a week**: Litma counts the words you add to any work.
- **Streak:** the days in a row you have written. Today doesn't break it until the day is over.
- **Rest days that keep the streak:** allow one or two days a week without writing, so a day off doesn't undo a good run.
- **This work:** in a work, choose **Goal and timer** on the right-hand rail to set a word goal and a **Finish by** date. Litma tells you how many words a day that needs.

> **Phone:** In a work, tap the tools button and choose **Goals**. **Goals and statistics** is in **Settings** and on your profile.

## The writing timer {#timer}

Write without stopping for 10, 15, 25, 45 or 60 minutes.

1. In a work, choose **Goal and timer** on the right-hand rail.
2. Pick how long and choose **Start**. The time left shows at the top of the editor.
3. When time is up, a soft chime and a card tell you how many words you wrote. Choose **Another** to go on.

Pause or stop it in the same panel. Your favourite length can be set in **Goals and statistics → Writing timer**.

> **Phone:** The phone's own notification tells you when time is up, even with Litma in the background.

## A daily reminder {#reminders}

In **Goals and statistics → Daily reminder**, tick **Remind me to write**, choose the time and the days. The reminder only comes on days your goal isn't met yet.

- **Website:** choose **Allow notifications on this device**. The reminder comes while Litma is open in a browser tab.
- **Desktop app:** the reminder comes while the app is running, even with its window closed on a Mac.
- **Phone:** the phone itself rings the reminder, even when Litma is closed. Allow notifications when the phone asks.

## Your writing statistics {#statistics}

**Goals and statistics** shows the last 7, 30 or 90 days: words written, time spent writing, writing days, words on a writing day and your best day, a chart of words per day, the hours you write most, and words by writing type and by work.

> **Good to know:** Litma keeps your writing statistics for the last 90 days, then lets them go. Your works themselves are kept for good. Your best streak is remembered.
