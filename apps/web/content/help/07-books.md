A book puts one or more works together with the pages a printed book needs: cover, title page, copyright page, contents and back cover. When it's ready, Litma gives you a PDF you can send to a printer.

## Make a book {#make-a-book}

1. On the desk open the **Books** tab and choose **New book**.
   ![The Books tab on the desk](shots/desk-books.webp)
2. Give it a title. The book editor opens with sample text in every field, so you edit instead of filling blanks.
3. Under **Book contents** choose **Add** and pick your works, and any book pages you want: half-title, title page, copyright, dedication, മേൽക്കുറിപ്പ് (epigraph), contents, a part divider or a page of your own text.
4. Drag items into order. Click an item to change it: for a work you can give it a different chapter title in the book, choose whether to **Print a chapter heading**, and untick **List in the table of contents**.

![The book editor](shots/book.webp "Book contents on the left, the cover designer in the middle, book details on the right.")

> **Good to know:** A book shows your works as they are now. Change a chapter in the work and the book has the change too.

## Book details {#book-details}

On the right of the book editor (the **Details** tab on the phone):

![Book details](shots/book-details.webp)

- **Title**, **Subtitle** and **Author.** Each has a **മലയാളം / As typed** switch: in മലയാളം mode you can type Manglish and it turns into Malayalam.
- **Publisher**, **Year**, **Edition** and **ISBN.** These go on the title page, the copyright page and the covers.
- **Page & type** for the book: page size, body font, size, line spacing, margins, and whether chapters start on a right-hand page.

> **Note:** The sample ISBN is all zeros. Replace it with your own before you print, or clear it.

## Design the cover {#cover-designer}

The cover designer is in the middle of the book editor. The big preview shows the front cover exactly as it will print.

![The cover designer](shots/cover-designer.webp "The live preview beside the presets. Click a part of the cover to size it, drag it to move it.")

1. **Presets:** pick one of the ten finished looks (Classic, Epic, Romance, Noir, Space, Gothic, Harbour, Modern, Paper, Bold). Each sets the layout, background, colours, title font and decoration. You can change any of them afterwards.
2. **Font size:** click a part of the cover (the title, the author, the publisher's name…) and use the slider to make it bigger or smaller.
3. **Layout:** where the title and author sit: Top, Upper, Left, Bottom, Big type or Centre. **Frame** adds a printed border.
4. **Background:** one of eight artworks drawn for book covers (fantasy, thriller, romance, science fiction, horror, literary, historical, self-help) or a plain colour.
5. **Title font:** Malayalam fonts including Karumbi, Dyuthi, Uroob, Meera and Rachana, plus effects like a shadow or a box behind the title.
6. **Extras:** a divider (line or flourish), an edition badge and a publisher mark.

### Move things on the cover

Drag the title, author, publisher's name, badge or mark on the preview to place them exactly. Use the arrow keys to nudge the part you picked (hold <kbd>Shift</kbd> for bigger steps). Picking a preset or a layout puts everything back in its place.

> **Tip:** The publisher's name sits on a soft band of the cover colour whenever there is artwork behind it, so it stays readable on dark corners of a picture. Leave the Publisher field empty to leave it off.

> **Phone:** In a book tap the cover to open the designer. Presets, layouts, fonts and backgrounds are the same; drag parts with your finger.

![The cover designer on the phone](shots/phone-cover.webp)

## The back cover {#back-cover}

Scroll down in the cover designer to the back cover:

- **Blurb:** the few lines that make someone want to read the book.
- **About the author** with an author picture (your first letter or one of Litma's avatars).
- A white strip at the bottom with the publisher (Litma Publishing House until you name your own), the genre, the cover credit, website, price and a barcode made from the ISBN.

The back repeats the front's artwork, softened, so the two sides belong together.

## Export a print-ready PDF {#book-pdf}

1. In the book editor choose **Preview & export PDF**.
2. The preview shows every page. Check it through.
3. Choose **Save as PDF**.
4. In the print window choose **Save as PDF** as the printer, set **Margins** to **None**, and turn on **Background graphics** so the covers print in colour.

![The book preview](shots/print.webp "In the print dialog choose Save as PDF, margins None, and turn on Background graphics.")

> **Phone:** In a book tap **Export** and pick **Whole book (PDF)** for one file with the covers and pages, or the two files a printer usually asks for: the pages, and the cover.
