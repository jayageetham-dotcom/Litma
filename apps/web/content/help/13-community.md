Litma Community is a place for writers who use Litma to talk to each other about writing, words and Litma itself. Open it from the menu under your picture, from Settings, or at getlitma.in/community/. You need to be signed in to read and take part.

## Start a conversation {#community-start}

Ask a question, share how you work, or ask for a reader's view of a short passage.

1. Open **Community** and choose **Start a conversation**.
2. Pick a category: Writers' corner, Craft of writing, Language and words, Read my work, or Litma help and ideas.
3. Give it a title and write your post. Use the **മലയാളം** switch above a box to turn Manglish into Malayalam as you type, or **As typed** to leave your words as they are.
4. Choose **Post**. The first time, you are asked to read and agree to the Community Guidelines.

Your post shows your pen name (or your profile name) and your avatar. Set a pen name in your profile if you prefer not to show your real name.

### Reply, like and edit

- Write in the box under a conversation and choose **Post** to reply.
- Choose the heart to like a post. Others see how many likes a post has, not who liked it.
- **Edit** and **Delete** appear on your own posts. You can edit while the conversation is open. A deleted post disappears for everyone.

### Find a conversation

Pick a category on the left (on a phone, swipe the row of categories), or type in the search box. Search understands Manglish, so "novalile" finds നോവലിലെ.

## Report a post {#community-report}

If a post breaks the guidelines (hate, harassment, someone's private details, copied work, spam), choose **Report** under it, pick what is wrong and send. The writer never sees who reported it. Litma's moderators look at every report.

## Block a writer {#community-block}

If you don't want to see someone's posts, choose **Block** under any conversation or reply they wrote, then **Block** again. Their conversations and replies disappear for you everywhere in the community, and they aren't told. Blocking only changes what you see; if they broke the guidelines, report the post too so the moderators can act.

To see who you've blocked, or to unblock someone, choose **Blocked writers** under the categories (getlitma.in/community/?view=blocked).

## How moderation works {#community-moderation}

Litma's moderators can close a conversation so no one can reply, remove a conversation or a reply, pin a conversation to the top, and stop a writer from posting for a time. A closed or removed conversation shows the reason.

If you are stopped from posting, you can still read the community, and a note tells you until when. For serious or repeated abuse, such as spam, Litma can also block the whole account from signing in, or delete it (see [If your account is blocked](/help/?a=blocked)). If you think a decision was wrong, write to support@getlitma.in.

Read the full [Community Guidelines](/community/guidelines/).
