Make Litma yours: your name on every new work, the picture beside it, the language of the menus and how it looks.

## Your profile {#profile}

Open your picture at the top right and choose **Profile** (on the phone, tap **Me**).

- **Name** and **Pen name.** Your pen name, or your name if you have none, fills the author line (രചന) of every new work.
- **Username**, **Location**, **About you** and **Website.**
- **Daily word goal:** the number of words you aim for each day. The desk shows how you are doing.
- **Default typing for new works:** Manglish, മലയാളം or English.
- **Your writing:** works, words, words today, your streak, your best day, a chart of the last 20 weeks and your words by writing type.

Under **Account** you can set a new password.

![Your profile on the phone](shots/phone-profile.webp)

## Your picture {#avatar}

Pick a picture to show beside your name and on the back cover of your books.

1. In **Profile**, choose one of Litma's illustrated avatars, or **Letter** to show the first letter of your name.
2. Choose a **Colour** for the letter or the background.
3. **Shuffle** shows a different set of pictures.

## Language, dark mode and other settings {#settings}

![Settings](shots/settings.webp)

Open **Settings** (the gear, or your picture → **Settings**):

- **Language:** the language of Litma's menus and buttons on this device. Your writing is not changed.
- **Dark mode:** the moon button at the top of the desk and the editor. Pages still print black on white.
- **Usage statistics:** Litma counts which pages and buttons are used (never your writing, titles or names) to learn what to improve. Switch it off here if you prefer.
- **Your AI keys** and **Connect AI assistants (MCP):** see [AI and assistants](/help/?c=ai).
- **About:** the version of Litma you are using.

## Get help {#get-help}

- **These guides:** on the website and desktop app, **Settings → Help and guide**, your picture → **Help and guide**, or **Help → Litma Help** in the desktop app's menu bar. On the phone, **Settings → Help and guide**. They open in your browser at getlitma.in/help.
- **Write to us:** support@getlitma.in. Tell us what you were doing, what you expected and which device you use (website, desktop app or phone). A screenshot helps a lot.

> **Tip:** If something looks wrong after an update, reload the page (or restart the desktop app). Your writing is saved on the device and in your account.
