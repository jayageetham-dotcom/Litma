You don't need a Malayalam keyboard to write in Litma. Type the way you would in a chat, in English letters, and Litma turns it into Malayalam.

## Type in Manglish {#manglish}

Manglish means writing Malayalam words in English letters: `mazha` for മഴ, `ezhuthu` for എഴുത്ത്. Litma turns each word into Malayalam as you finish it.

![Suggestions under a Manglish word](shots/manglish.webp "Type a word; Litma offers the Malayalam spellings, best first.")

1. Make sure **Manglish** is chosen at the left of the toolbar.
2. Type a word in English letters, for example `nammude`.
3. A list of Malayalam spellings appears under the word, best first.
4. Press <kbd>Space</kbd>, <kbd>Enter</kbd> or a punctuation mark to take the highlighted spelling.

### Pick another spelling

- Press a number (<kbd>1</kbd> to <kbd>6</kbd>) to take that row, or click it.
- Use <kbd>↑</kbd> <kbd>↓</kbd> to move through the list. When there are more than six spellings, <kbd>Page Up</kbd> / <kbd>Page Down</kbd> or the small arrows at the bottom show the next set.
- Press <kbd>Esc</kbd>, or choose the **Aa** row, to keep the word in English letters.

### Words you type in the middle of a line

If you go back and type a word between other words, it turns into Malayalam when you move the cursor away from it or click somewhere else. You don't have to type a space after it.

> **Tip:** Spell the way it sounds. Double letters make long or doubled sounds: `kaalam` → കാലം, `amma` → അമ്മ. Capitals mark the harder sounds: `Ta` → ട, `La` → ള, `Na` → ണ.

> **Phone:** On the phone the spellings sit in a strip above the keyboard that you can scroll sideways. A space takes the first one; tap any other to use it. The first item in quotes keeps the English word.

## Switch between Manglish, Malayalam and English {#input-modes}

The three buttons at the left of the toolbar set how your typing is read:

![Manglish, മലയാളം and English buttons](shots/toolbar.webp "Manglish, മലയാളം and English sit at the left of the toolbar.")

- **Manglish:** English letters turn into Malayalam.
- **മലയാളം:** type straight in Malayalam with a Malayalam keyboard. See [Malayalam keyboards](#malayalam-keyboard).
- **English:** plain English typing; nothing is converted.

Press <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> + <kbd>M</kbd> (<kbd>⌘</kbd> + <kbd>M</kbd> on a Mac) to go from one to the next. The mode shows at the bottom right of the editor too.

> **Good to know:** Text you type in English mode stays English. Litma never converts it later.

You can choose the mode new works start in: on the website and desktop in **Profile → Default typing for new works**, on the phone in **Settings → Typing for new works**.

## Use a Malayalam keyboard {#malayalam-keyboard}

If you already type Malayalam with a keyboard layout, choose **മലയാളം** on the toolbar.

![The Malayalam keyboard panel](shots/keyboard.webp "The Malayalam keyboard panel shows the layout and every key.")

Litma has its own **InScript (Enhanced)** keyboard built in. It is the standard Government of India layout, the same one Windows, macOS and Linux offer, and it works even on a computer with no Malayalam keyboard installed:

1. Choose **മലയാളം** on the toolbar.
2. Open **Malayalam keyboard** on the right-hand rail, pick **InScript (Enhanced)** and choose **Show keyboard map** to see every key.
3. Type. Each key gives the Malayalam letter shown on the map.

To make a chillu (ൻ ർ ൽ ൾ ൺ), type the consonant, then the virama (്), then <kbd>]</kbd>.

If your computer or phone already has a Malayalam keyboard, pick **Your device keyboard** in the same panel and Litma leaves the typing to it. (If your computer already types Malayalam, Litma leaves those keys alone either way.) Phones and tablets use the device keyboard by default.

## Turn Manglish you already typed into Malayalam {#convert-selection}

Typed a passage in English mode by mistake, or pasted Manglish from somewhere else?

1. Select the text.
2. Press <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> + <kbd>Shift</kbd> + <kbd>M</kbd> (<kbd>⌘</kbd> + <kbd>Shift</kbd> + <kbd>M</kbd> on a Mac), or choose the **convert** button on the toolbar (the one that reads "Convert selected Manglish to Malayalam" when you point at it).

Each word becomes its first Malayalam spelling. Read it through afterwards; a few words may need a different spelling.

## Manglish in book details and other fields {#manglish-fields}

Some fields outside the editor, like a book's Title, Subtitle and Author, have a small **മലയാളം / As typed** switch.

- **മലയാളം:** each word you type turns into Malayalam when you finish it, as in the editor.
- **As typed:** the text stays exactly as you type it, for English names or titles.

Litma remembers your choice for each field on that device.
