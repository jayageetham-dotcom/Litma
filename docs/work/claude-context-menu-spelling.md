### Spelling engine `MlSpeller.quoted()`
- PASS (a) chains: 53 bases (negatives in -ില്ല, -ആവില്ല, -ഏണ്ട, past participles) × എന്ന്/എന്നു/എന്ന/എന്നും/എന്നോ = 315 words: 201 already ok with mlmorph alone, **104 newly ok**, the only ones still flagged are not words (പെരുന്നാൾെന്ന്) or an unknown compound base (നോമ്പുനോറ്റെന്ന്). കാണാനില്ലെന്ന്ന് and ലെന്ന് stay flagged. Small correction to the build note: കാണാനാവില്ലെന്ന് was already ok before.
- PASS (c) prose: 33 of 345 words (9.6%) flagged by mlmorph alone, **22 (6.4%) after**; the 11 fixed are all quotative chains (ഒരുങ്ങിയില്ലെന്ന്, കാണാനില്ലെന്ന്, പോകേണ്ടെന്ന്, കുറഞ്ഞില്ലെന്ന്, വായിക്കുന്നില്ലെന്ന്, ജയിച്ചില്ലെന്ന്, വൈകില്ലെന്നും, കണ്ടില്ലെന്ന്, വിഷമിക്കേണ്ടെന്ന്, തികഞ്ഞില്ലെന്ന്, കണ്ടിട്ടില്ലെന്ന്). No word got worse.
- **FAIL (b) false passes, → web-dev:** quoted() trusts any base mlmorph accepts, and mlmorph accepts many misspelt bases as odd noun compounds. Of 957 misspellings of 53 chain words (vowel length, ന/ണ, ല/ള, ര/റ, missing ്, missing doubling), mlmorph alone flags 811; **quoted() lets 122 of those through (15%)**. Realistic examples: വരിലെന്ന്, കണ്ടിലെന്ന്, വന്നിലെന്ന്, ചെയ്തിലെന്ന്, പണമിലെന്ന്, ഒന്നുമിലെന്ന് (ല്ല written ല: the most likely slip in exactly these words), മരിചെന്ന്, ഇരുനെന്ന്, വെണ്ടെന്ന്, ഓടീയെന്ന്, പറയനാവില്ലെന്ന്. Second class: nouns ending in -a, which take യ (അമ്മയെന്ന്), are now accepted without it: അമ്മെന്ന്, ഉമ്മെന്ന്, സീതെന്ന്, ആയിഷെന്ന്, പുഴെന്ന്, സഭെന്ന് (all flagged before). Suggested fix (tried in a lab script, not in feature code): accept only when one of `morph.analyse(base)` ends in `<neg>` or `<adv-clause-rp-past>` (or is വേണ്ട `<neg>`). That keeps കാണാനില്ലെന്ന്, അറിയില്ലെന്ന്, വേണ്ടെന്ന്, മരിച്ചെന്ന് and rejects the examples above that analyse as `<n>`/`<np>` (all but പറയനാവില്ലെന്ന്-type slips, which mlmorph parses as a verb + ഇല്ല), but would lose പോകേണ്ടെന്ന്/വിഷമിക്കേണ്ടെന്ന് (mlmorph reads പോകേണ്ട as പോകുക+അണ്ടുക), so -ഏണ്ട needs its own case. Not blocking on its own (mlmorph already passes the bare misspelt bases, e.g. വരില), but it is a measurable loss of catches.
- Gap (not a regression): the same chains with other endings stay "Check spelling?": കാണാനില്ലെന്നാണ്, അറിയില്ലെന്നുള്ള, പറഞ്ഞില്ലെന്നത്, പോയില്ലെന്നായിരുന്നു, കിട്ടില്ലെന്നുമാണ് (269 of 630 such words). കിട്ടില്ലെന്നുമാണ് is in the prose sample and its first suggestion is the misspelling കിട്ടിലെന്നുമാണ്. എന്നാണ് and എന്നുള്ള are very common in news-style prose; worth adding to the ending list.
- Older false alarms seen in the prose (mlmorph, not this branch): ചേട്ടൻ (suggests ചേടൻ), നാട്ടുകാർ, വീട്ടുകാരും, പുലർച്ചെ, അധികൃതർ, പെരുന്നാളിന്, ക്രിസ്മസിന്, ഇഷ്ടമുള്ളവർ, പെയ്യുന്നുണ്ടായിരുന്നു.

### Web right-click menu
Novel editor, 1440×900 and 1024×768, light and dark: **60 of 62 checks pass in each of the four runs** (script `/tmp/tester/suite.py`; screenshots `/tmp/tester/<w>-<theme>-*.png`).
- PASS: opens on a word, stays on screen (flips up near the bottom, left near the right edge; synthetic right-clicks at the bottom-right and top-right corners stay inside the window), focus stays in the text; Esc closes with the text unchanged; ↓ goes to Paste (first enabled), End/Home, ↑ and ↓ wrap, Enter on Keyboard shortcuts opens the dialog, Enter with nothing active only closes; right-click inside a selection keeps it, outside moves the cursor; Cut/Copy disabled without a selection.
- PASS each item, then Ctrl+Z: Cut (clipboard + removed, undo restores), Copy, Paste, Paste without formatting (HTML `<b>` on the clipboard pastes plain; Paste keeps `<strong>`), Delete, Select all, Bold/Italic/Underline (and Bold shows as on when on), Clear formatting (undo brings both marks back), Look up / Find rhymes (select the word under the pointer and open the panel), Ask AI, Keyboard shortcuts.
- PASS: right after the menu closes, Manglish shows its list and `amma␣` becomes അമ്മ, `kadal␣` after an item becomes കടൽ; any other key closes the menu and types; page wheel closes it; Shift + right-click shows no Litma menu; 390 px phone (touch) and a 390 px mouse window show no Litma menu.
- PASS spelling in the menu (local mode, a broken-letter issue injected by typing അവന്‍ with the old ZWJ chillu): "Broken letter" heading, fix അവൻ first, Ignore, no Keep; the fix applies and Ctrl+Z restores. Engine verdicts (suspect/mistake with Keep) can't be shown in local mode: not tested.
- **FAIL, blocking for keyboard users, → web-dev: the menu key and Shift+F10 open the menu and it closes at once.** Steps: click in the body line, press Shift+F10 (or the ContextMenu key). Trace: `contextmenu` → menu mounts → Chromium itself scrolls `.desk-scroll` by 32 px (it does this for a keyboard context menu even when the event is cancelled; checked with a capture listener that cancels it first) → the `document` scroll listener calls `close()`. Shift+F10 failed in all four runs; the menu key passed in the suite only by timing and failed when run alone (`/tmp/tester/f10b.py`, `f10c.py`). Also note Chromium sends the keyboard event with the caret's coordinates and `button -1`, not 0,0, so the `keyboard` branch in `onMenu` is never taken (it still works, as a click at the caret). Suggested: ignore scrolls for a short moment after opening, or move the menu with the scroll instead of closing, and detect the keyboard case by `button !== 2`.
- Risk (not reproducible in Chromium): `if (e.shiftKey) return` would also swallow a keyboard Shift+F10 in browsers that set shiftKey on that event; Chromium sends shiftKey=false.
- **FAIL, minor, → web-dev: in a short window the lower items can't be reached.** Steps: window 1024×420 (or any window shorter than the menu: 520 px plain, 617 px with spelling fixes, so e.g. a 1366×768 laptop at 125% zoom with a marked word), right-click a word, scroll the wheel inside the menu: the menu's own scroll reaches the `document` scroll listener (capture) and closes it. With the keyboard, End makes Keyboard shortcuts active but it is drawn below the window (458–490 px in a 420 px window): the active item is not scrolled into view. Screenshot `/tmp/tester/1024x420-menu.png`.
- Note (cosmetic, designer's call): when the menu is taller than the space both above and below the click (spelling fixes at 1440×900), it is pinned at the top and covers the clicked line to the right of the pointer (`/tmp/tester/1440x900-spell-menu.png`).
